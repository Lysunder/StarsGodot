# ComputerPlayers

`core/ai/computer_players.gd`, extends `RefCounted`

The AI step (spec S22 "The AI step"): before a turn is generated, every computer player still
in the game that has no orders of its own makes them, in player order, drawing from the classic
stream; so does every inactive human player, with the INACTIVE personality (housekeeping only).
Each sees only its own view of the game, and its orders are checked and applied with everyone's
when the turn is generated.

## Constants

- `const PERSONALITY := "ai_personality"`
- `const INACTIVE := "inactive"`: The personality that plays for a human player who is inactive (not offered for new players).
- `const LEVELS := ["easy", "standard", "harder", "expert"]`: The difficulty levels, easiest first (Player.ai_level).
- `const RACES := UniverseGenerator.AI_PERSONALITIES`: The original's personalities, whose built-in races (one per level, content `races`) every personality plays: a built-in one its own, one of the player's those it names.
- `const DEFAULT_RACES := "robotoids"`: The races a personality plays when its brain has none of its own (the baseline brain).
- `const FILES_WRITTEN := 2`: Files the original writes for each computer player after it plays (log, history), each drawing a cipher salt (S01 "Draws outside the rules").
- `const FILE_SALT_RANGE := 2000`
