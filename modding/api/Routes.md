# Routes

`core/rules/routes.gd`, extends `RefCounted`

Routes and default orders (spec S11 "Route"): a fleet idle at a planet with a route, or newly
built there (S09), gets a waypoint at the route's destination; other idle fleets with mining
robots get the remote mining or merge task.

## Constants

- `const DOCK_WARP := 9`: With a dock at the destination the warp is chosen from this one down by fuel range.
- `const EASE_MIN := 2`: The lowest warp the ease-off step comes down to.
- `const DISTANCE_ROUND_UP := 0.999`: The route distance is rounded up by this, then truncated.
- `const DEFAULT_PLAN := 0`
