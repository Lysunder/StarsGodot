# Battles

`core/battle/battles.gd`, extends `RefCounted`

The turn's battles (spec S16): at the start of the waypoint 1 step, every place where hostile
fleets meet fights a battle in turn; each is recorded in `state.battles`, then its results
follow (S16 "Battle results"): the messages, the tech bonus tries, an Alternate Reality planet
emptied by the loss of its starbase, and the "fought this turn" marks. Destroyed fleets go when
all are done.

## Constants

- `const LOSS_LEFT := 0x4000`: The battle's loss table (per owner × 16 + design slot): BattleEngine.LOSS_HIT, LOSS_LEFT (ships left) and the ships killed in the low bits.
- `const LOSS_KILLED := 0x1FFF`
- `const SLOTS := 16`
- `const STARBASE_SLOT := 16`
- `const DESIGN_REF := 32`: A design as the messages name it: owner × 32 + slot.
- `const MANY_COLONISTS := 1000`: An Alternate Reality race that loses more colonists (hundreds) than this gets the "many" text.
- `const TWO := 2`
- `const FLEET_WORD := 512`
