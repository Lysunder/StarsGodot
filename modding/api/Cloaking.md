# Cloaking

`core/rules/cloaking.gd`, extends `RefCounted`

Cloaking (spec S15 "Cloaking"): a fleet's and a starbase's cloak percent, and how a scanner's
reach shrinks against them.

## Constants

- `const PCT := 100`
- `const STARBASE_MAX_UNITS := 25000`: Starbase cloak units above this give no cloak at all (`Design_CloakUnits`).
- `const NO_CLOAK_FACTOR := 10000`: K = (100 − cloak)²; K at or above this means "not cloaked".
