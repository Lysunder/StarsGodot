# ProductionCosts

`core/rules/production_costs.gd`, extends `RefCounted`

What production items cost a player (spec S09 steps 1-2b). A cost is [ironium, boranium,
germanium, resources].

Trait parameters (S09 "Mod hooks"): `production.miniaturization_per_level` / `_max` and
`production.cost_at_tech_level_pct` (BET), `cost.<category>_adjust_pct`,
`cost.tag.<tag>_adjust_pct` and `cost.<category>_resources_pct` (part prices by kind),
`production.defense_cost_pct` (IS), `production.alchemy_cost` (MA), `production.terraform_cost`
(TT) and `production.terraform_cost_pct` (CA), the packet costs (PP, IT), and
`production.starbase_adjust_pct` (ISB, AR).

## Constants

- `const MINERALS := ["ironium", "boranium", "germanium"]`
- `const RESOURCES := 3`
- `const UPGRADE_CREDIT := [8, 7]`: Credit for a replaced part in a starbase upgrade: [similar part, other kind of part], tenths.

A part's or hull's price for a player (S09 step 2). `def` is the part or hull definition.
`plain`: without `production.cost_at_tech_level_pct` (the battle salvage figure, S16).
`tech`: the tech levels to price at (the player's own when empty).

### `upgrade_cost()`

```gdscript
static func upgrade_cost(new: Design, old: Design, player: Player, content: ContentRegistry, tech: Array[int] = []) -> Array[int]
```

The price of a starbase design built where `old` stands (S09 step 2b), before the starbase
adjustments of step 1. The designs' prices are at `tech` (the stored stats, priced at the turn's
start; empty: the current levels), the hull and the parts credited at the current levels.

### `unit_cost()`

```gdscript

```

One unit of a queue item on a planet (S09 step 1). Designs are priced at `tech`: the turn's
generation passes the levels at its start, which the original's stored design stats hold (a
level gained during the turn doesn't change them); empty: the current levels.
