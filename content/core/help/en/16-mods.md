# Mods

The game can be changed with mods: new parts and hulls, changed rules, new events, other looks and wording. Open
**Mods...** in the main menu to turn mods on or off and to set their order.

- **Gameplay mods** change the rules. Every player in a game needs the same ones, so a saved game remembers which
  were on and turns them on again when it's loaded.
- **Cosmetic mods** change only what you see: pictures, text and fonts. Each player can choose their own.
- A mod with scripts asks for your trust before it runs. Only trust mods from people you trust.

Mods are folders in your user data folder, under `mods`. The game's modding guide explains how to make one.

## The classic look

If you own a copy of the original Stars!, the game can import its pictures and wording from your copy as a
cosmetic mod for your own use. Choose **Import Classic Look...** in the main menu and point it at the folder you
installed it in. Nothing is copied anywhere but your own computer, and the game plays the same with or without it.
