# Ship design

Open the designer with {key:ship_design} (or **Commands > Ship Design**). It has three lists: your ship designs,
your starbase designs, and the hulls and parts you could use.

## Making a design

- Pick a hull. Its slots appear in the picture; each slot takes certain kinds of parts and holds up to a number of
  them.
- Choose **Copy** with a hull selected to start a new design on it, or with a design selected to start from
  that design.
- Drag parts from the list into the slots, or double-click a part to put it in the first slot that takes it.
  Drag a part out of a slot, or right-click it, to take it out. Some slots must be filled, such as a ship's
  engine slots.
- The totals beside the picture show the design's cost, mass, armor, shields, speed, fuel, cargo and weapons as
  you go.
- Name the design and choose **OK**. It can then be built at any planet that can build it.

New hulls and parts become available as your tech levels rise (see [[research]]). Some parts only exist for races
with certain traits (see [[races]]).

## Ships and starbases

Starbases are designed the same way, from starbase hulls, and are built by the planet they will orbit. A starbase
with a dock can build ships; a larger dock can build larger ships.

## Changing a design

**Edit** changes a design. A design with ships flying can't be changed: copy it and change the copy instead.
**Delete** removes a design, and every ship built to it goes with it, so be careful.
