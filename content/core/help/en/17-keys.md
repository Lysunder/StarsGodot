# Keyboard shortcuts

These keys work anywhere on the game screen. You can change them in **View > Settings**.

{keys}

## On the map

- **Mouse wheel:** zoom in and out.
- **Right or middle drag:** move the map.
- **Click:** select; click again to step through what is there.
- **Right-click:** a menu of what is at that spot.
- **Shift-click:** add a waypoint for the selected fleet.
- **Escape:** cancel picking a planet.

## In lists and dialogs

- **Delete:** remove the selected waypoint.
- **Shift** and **Ctrl** with the production and cargo buttons: 10 or 100 at a time (both together in the cargo
  dialog: 1,000).
