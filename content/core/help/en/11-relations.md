# Other players

You meet other races as your scanners find them. The **Player Relations** window ({key:relations}) lists every
race you know, and lets you set how you treat each one:

- **Friend:** your fleets don't attack theirs, and theirs can use your starbases' gates and repair.
- **Neutral:** your fleets fight theirs only if your battle plans say to attack neutrals.
- **Enemy:** your armed fleets attack theirs when they meet.

Relations are one-sided: a race you call a friend may still call you an enemy.

## Others' fleets

Fleets of other players that your scanners can see this year are drawn on the map. Click one to see its owner,
ships and mass in the summary pane. You can make a fleet follow or chase one by Shift-clicking it as a waypoint.
The **Others' Fleets** report lists all of them.

## Giving things away

A fleet's Transfer task gives the fleet to another player. Cargo can also be handed to another player's fleet or
planet.
