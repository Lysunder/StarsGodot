# Getting started

StarsGodot is a turn-based game of galactic expansion. You lead one race from a single home planet. Explore the
stars around you, settle the worlds your people can live on, out-research and out-build your rivals, and win by
the goals set when the game was created.

## A year at a time

Each turn is one year. During a turn you give orders: what your planets build, where your fleets fly and what they
do when they arrive, which technology your scientists study, and how your race treats the others. Nothing happens
until the turn is generated. Then every player's orders are carried out together, in a fixed order, and you get a
new year, with messages about what happened.

- Press {key:generate} (or **Turn > Generate**) when your orders are ready.
- In a game with other human players, the year is generated once everyone has submitted. See [[modes]].

## Your first few years

- Look at your home planet in the [[planets|planet tiles]] on the left. It starts with mines, factories and a
  starbase.
- Open its production queue and keep it busy: factories and mines grow your economy; scouts find new worlds;
  colony ships settle them. See [[production]].
- Send your scout out to look at the stars nearby. Select it, then Shift-click a planet on the map to add a
  waypoint. See [[fleets]].
- Set a research field and how much of your resources goes to research. See [[research]].
- Read your messages: they report what your scanners saw, what was built and what needs your attention. See
  [[messages]].

Planets with a high value (green on the map's Value view) are where your colonists will grow best. See [[scanner]]
for the map's views.
