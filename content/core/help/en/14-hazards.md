# Hazards and wonders

## Minefields

Fleets with mine layers can lay minefields. A fleet moving through an enemy minefield may hit a mine, take damage
and be stopped; flying slowly lowers the chance. Fleets with beam weapons or mine sweepers sweep mines away. Fields
shrink a little each year.

## Stargates

Planets whose starbases have stargates can send fleets instantly to another gate, within the gates' limits on
range and ship mass. Going beyond those limits is possible but can destroy ships.

## Mass drivers and packets

A starbase with a mass driver can fling minerals at another planet as a packet. The receiving planet needs a mass
driver of its own to catch it safely; an uncaught packet hits the planet hard.

## Wormholes

Wormholes connect two points in space. A fleet that reaches one end comes out of the other. Wormholes drift, and
now and then their ends jump to new places.

## Salvage

Ships that are scrapped in space or destroyed leave salvage that fleets can pick up as cargo.

## The Mystery Trader

A strange ship that crosses the galaxy from time to time. Meet it with a fleet carrying enough minerals and it may
give you something rare in return.

## Random events

If the game allows them, comets, mineral discoveries, research breakthroughs, artifacts and other events happen now
and then.
