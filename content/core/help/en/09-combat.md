# Combat

## Battles

A battle starts when fleets of players who are enemies meet at the same place and at least one of them is armed
and willing to attack. Battles are fought on a small grid over several rounds. Each round, ships move in order of
their initiative, and weapons fire at targets in range: beams lose strength over distance, torpedoes can miss.
Shields absorb damage before armor; a ship whose armor is gone is destroyed.

## Battle plans

A battle plan tells a fleet what to shoot first, which secondary targets to accept, how to fight (for example, to
close in, to keep its distance, or to run away) and whom to attack. Edit your plans with {key:battle_plans} (or
**Commands > Battle Plans**) and pick a fleet's plan in its Battle plan tile.

## Who is an enemy

Player relations decide whom your fleets fight. Open them with {key:relations} (or **Commands > Player Relations**)
and mark each player as a friend, neutral or an enemy. See [[relations]].

## Watching a battle

After a battle you get a message about it. The battle viewer replays it round by round, and the battle report
lists every battle you took part in this year.

## Bombing and invasion

Fleets with bombs over an enemy planet bomb it each year, killing colonists and destroying installations; the
planet's defenses shoot some of the bombs down. Ships carrying colonists can invade an enemy planet by unloading
them there: your troops fight the planet's defenders, and if they win, the planet is yours.
