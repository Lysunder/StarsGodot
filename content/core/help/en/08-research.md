# Research

Your race studies six fields: **energy**, **weapons**, **propulsion**, **construction**, **electronics** and
**biotechnology**. Each has a level; higher levels unlock new parts and hulls and make building them cheaper.

Open the research window with {key:research} (or **Commands > Research**).

## What you can set

- **Resources for research:** the share of every planet's resources that goes to research each year. The rest goes
  to production.
- **Current field:** the field your research goes into now.
- **Next field:** what to study once the current level is reached: a field you choose, the same field again, or
  whichever field has the lowest level.

The window shows each field's level, how much you've put toward the next level, and an estimate of how long it
will take. Fields your race is good at cost less; your race's research costs are set in the race wizard (see
[[races]]).

Leftover resources from planets with nothing left to build can also go to research (see [[production]]).
Scrapping alien ships, capturing planets and battle can sometimes teach you a level too.
