# The game screen

The screen has four panes, and most of the game is played in them.

## Command pane (left)

Tiles for the planet or fleet you have selected, in two columns. Each tile has a title bar; click its arrow to fold
the tile up or open it again. Drag a tile by its title bar onto another tile to put it there, or below a column's
tiles to put it at the bottom of that column; the game remembers where you put them. For a planet you see its
status, production, starbase and routes (hold the mouse button down on the starbase tile to see its design); for a
fleet, its ships, cargo, waypoints, the task at the selected waypoint, and its battle plan. The arrow buttons next
to the name step through your planets or fleets.

## Scanner (centre)

The map of the galaxy, as far as your scanners and records know it. See [[scanner]].

## Messages (bottom left)

This year's messages, one at a time, with buttons to step through them and to jump to what a message is about.
See [[messages]].

## Selection summary (bottom right)

A short summary of whatever is selected on the map: for a planet, its value, how old your report on it is, its
population, environment and minerals; for a fleet, its ships, fuel, cargo, mass and next waypoint. Hold the left
mouse button on a fleet's picture to see its ships, or the right button to see its main design.

## Menus

- **File:** save, close the game, quit.
- **View:** the scanner's views and overlays, and Settings (fonts, interface size, colours, keys).
- **Turn:** generate the turn (or submit your orders, in a game with other players).
- **Commands:** ship design, research, battle plans, player relations.
- **Report:** tables of your planets, your fleets and others' fleets, battles, and the score.
- **Help:** this help, and the version you are running.

Keyboard shortcuts are listed in [[keys]].
