# Races

Before a game you can design your own race in the race wizard, or play one of the ready-made races. A race is a
balance of strengths and weaknesses, paid for with **advantage points**: strong choices cost points, weak ones give
points back, and the race must not end up with fewer than zero.

## What a race is made of

- **Primary racial trait:** the race's way of life, such as long-range traders, shapeshifters or warmongers. It
  decides much about how the race plays.
- **Lesser racial traits:** smaller advantages and drawbacks, such as better engines or cheaper research.
- **Environment:** the gravity, temperature and radiation the race prefers, as ranges. Wider ranges make more
  planets worth settling; a race can also be immune to one of them.
- **Growth rate:** how fast colonists multiply.
- **Economy:** how many colonists run a factory, how many resources factories and mines produce, and what they
  cost.
- **Research costs:** cheaper or dearer for each of the six fields, with an option to start some fields at level 3.

The wizard shows the advantage points left as you change things, and how many planets your race could expect to
find habitable. Races are saved as files you can use again.
