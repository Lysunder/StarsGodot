# Messages

Each year brings messages: what your planets built, what your scanners discovered, battles, research, random
events and more. They are shown one at a time in the messages pane.

- The arrow buttons step to the previous and next message.
- **Goto** jumps to what the message is about: it selects a planet or fleet on the map, or opens the research
  window, the designer, the battle viewer or the score.
- The box by each message shows whether its kind is shown or filtered out; click it to change that for the
  rest of the game. A magnifying glass appears while any of this year's messages are filtered.

Read your messages every year: they are how you learn that a colony was founded, a fleet ran out of fuel, a
minefield was hit or an enemy appeared.
