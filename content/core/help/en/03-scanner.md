# The scanner

The scanner is the map in the middle of the screen. Planets are circles; your fleets are triangles pointing toward
their next waypoint; other players' fleets are triangles in their own colour. Minefields show as faint discs, and
you may also see wormholes, mineral packets, salvage and the Mystery Trader.

## Moving around

- **Zoom:** the mouse wheel, or the zoom buttons on the toolbar.
- **Pan:** drag with the right or middle mouse button.
- **Select:** click a planet or fleet. Clicking the same spot again steps through everything there.
- **What's here:** right-click (without dragging) for a menu of the planet and every fleet at that spot.

The status bar under the map shows the position under the mouse, what is there, and how far it is from what you
have selected.

## Giving fleets orders on the map

With one of your fleets selected:

- **Shift-click** adds a waypoint after the selected one: a planet, a fleet (to follow it), or a point in space.
- **Drag** a waypoint to move it somewhere else.
- The fleet's path is drawn from waypoint to waypoint.

More about fleets and tasks in [[fleets]].

## Views and overlays

The toolbar above the map switches the view:

- **Normal:** planets in their owners' colours.
- **Minerals:** the minerals lying on each planet's surface.
- **Conc.:** how rich each planet's mineral deposits are.
- **Value:** how good each planet is for your race (see [[planets]]).
- **Pop.:** population.

**Names** writes planet names next to the planets. **Scan** shows your scanner coverage: normal scanners in red,
penetrating scanners (which also see fleets in orbit and planets' contents behind them) in green.
