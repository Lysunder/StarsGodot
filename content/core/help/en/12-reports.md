# Reports and score

The **Report** menu opens tables of your planets, your fleets, others' fleets you have seen, and this year's
battles. Press {key:reports} to open them and again for the next one.

- Click a column's label for its menu: sort by it, forward or reverse, or hide it (or show a hidden one).
  The sort you had before becomes the second level, which breaks ties.
- Click a row to put that planet or fleet under command. Some cells do more: a planet's Production cell opens
  its production queue, and a fleet's Cargo cell opens the cargo dialog.
- In the battle report, click a battle to select where it was fought, and again to watch it.

## Score

The score window ({key:score}) ranks every player and shows a graph of how the scores have changed. The score
counts planets, population, resources, tech levels, starbases and ships.

## Winning

The game's creator picks which victory conditions count, how many of them a player must meet, and how many years
must pass before anyone can win. The conditions include owning a share of all planets, reaching a tech level in
some fields, reaching a score, being well ahead of the second player, producing enough resources, having enough
capital ships, and having the highest score after a number of years. When a player wins, every player is told.

A player who has lost all planets and all ships is eliminated.
