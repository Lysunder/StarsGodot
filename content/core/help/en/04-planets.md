# Planets

Every planet has an environment (gravity, temperature and radiation), mineral deposits, and perhaps a population.

## Value

A planet's **value** says how well your race can live there, from your race's preferred ranges for gravity,
temperature and radiation (set in the race wizard, see [[races]]). A positive value means your colonists will
grow; the higher, the faster and the more of them the planet can hold. A negative value means the planet is
hostile: colonists there die off a little every year. Terraforming can move a planet's environment toward what
your race likes and raise its value.

## Population and growth

Colonists grow each year toward the planet's capacity. Growth slows as a planet fills up, and a planet over its
capacity loses people. Colonists are counted in hundreds; cargo holds carry them in units of 100.

## Minerals

There are three minerals: **ironium**, **boranium** and **germanium**. Each planet has a concentration of each (how
rich the deposits are) and minerals already mined lying on its surface. Mines dig minerals up each year; the richer
the deposit, the more they dig, and deposits slowly wear down as they are mined. Minerals pay, with resources, for
everything you build.

## Resources

Your colonists and your factories produce **resources** every year. Resources pay for production and research.
How many colonists run a factory, and how many resources each makes, depend on your race.

## Installations

- **Mines** dig minerals.
- **Factories** make resources.
- **Defenses** protect the planet against bombing and invasion.
- A **planetary scanner** lets the planet see fleets and planets around it.
- A **starbase** orbits the planet; it can repair ships, refuel them, build them (with a dock) and fight.

How many mines, factories and defenses a planet can run depends on its population. The planet tiles and the
summary show what a planet has and what it could have. See [[production]] to build them.
