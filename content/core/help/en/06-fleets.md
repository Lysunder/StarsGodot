# Fleets and waypoints

Ships fly in fleets. A fleet has a list of waypoints: waypoint 0 is where it is now, and each later waypoint is a
place to go, with the warp speed to fly there and a task to do on arrival.

## Moving

- Select a fleet and Shift-click on the map to add waypoints (see [[scanner]]).
- In the Waypoints tile, pick a waypoint to change its speed and task, or press **Delete** to remove it.
- Higher warp is faster but burns much more fuel. The tile shows the distance, the years it will take and the fuel
  you will have left; it warns you when the fleet will run out of fuel on the way.
- Fleets refuel at your planets' starbases, and some engines scoop fuel as they fly.
- Flying faster than warp 9 can damage ships that aren't built for it.

## Tasks

- **Transport:** load or unload cargo (minerals, colonists, fuel). The quick choices load or drop everything, or
  wait until the hold is full.
- **Colonize:** settle the planet with the colonists aboard a ship that has a colonization module.
- **Remote mining:** mine an uninhabited planet with a fleet that has mining robots.
- **Merge with fleet:** join another of your fleets there.
- **Scrap fleet:** take the ships apart; part of their minerals are recovered at the planet.
- **Lay mines:** lay a minefield with the fleet's mine layers.
- **Patrol:** look for enemy fleets nearby and go after them.
- **Route:** send the fleet on to the planet's route destination.
- **Transfer fleet:** give the fleet to another player.

A fleet can repeat its orders: when it reaches its last waypoint, it starts over from the first.

## Fleet tiles

- **Fleet composition:** the ships in the fleet; split ships off into new fleets, or merge fleets that are
  together.
- **Cargo:** what the fleet carries; the cargo dialog moves cargo between the fleet and a planet or another fleet
  in the same place.
- **Battle plan:** how the fleet behaves when a fight starts (see [[combat]]).
