# Production

Each of your planets builds from its own production queue, paid with that planet's resources and the minerals on
its surface. Open it from the planet's Production tile.

## The queue

- The left list shows what the planet can build; the right list is the queue, built from the top down.
- **Add** puts the selected item into the queue; **Remove** takes it out; the arrows move an item up or down.
- Hold **Shift** to add or remove 10 at a time, and **Ctrl** for 100.
- The cost of the selected item and an estimate of when each item will be finished are shown below the lists.

Anything the planet can't finish this year carries on next year, keeping what has been spent on it. When the
queue runs dry, leftover resources can go to research (the "contribute only leftover resources to research"
setting).

## Standing orders

Some items are **auto** items, such as Auto Mines, Auto Factories and Auto Max Terraform. They stay in the queue and
build up to the number you set each year, as long as there is something left to build, so a planet keeps growing
without you editing its queue every year.

## Ships and starbases

A planet with a starbase that has a dock can build ships; any of your planets can build or replace its starbase.
Your own designs appear in the list once you have made them in the ship designer (see [[design]]).

## Templates

The queue can be filled from a **production template**. The default template is what every new colony starts
with. You can keep a few more templates of your own and apply them to any planet.
